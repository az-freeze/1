Frequently Asked Questions
Why won't my dishwasher drain?
Drain problems can be caused by blocked filters, faulty pumps, or kinked hoses. We'll diagnose and repair the correct component.
Is it better to repair or replace a dishwasher?
We evaluate repair cost vs replacement based on age, part availability, and condition, and recommend the most cost-effective solution.
Do you handle installation after repair?
We can reinstall and test built-in units as part of the service when required.
What parts do you use?
We use OEM or high-quality aftermarket parts depending on availability and your preference.
What payment methods do you accept?
Major credit cards, debit and cash are accepted. Payment is collected after repairs unless other arrangements are made.
