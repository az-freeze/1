Frequently Asked Questions
Why won't my washer spin?
Common causes include lid switches, motor couplings, belts, or control board faults. We'll diagnose and explain options.
Do you replace seals and bearings?
Yes. We replace worn seals and bearings and test the drum and suspension to ensure proper operation.
What if my washer shows an error code?
Provide the code to the technician; many codes point to specific failures we can diagnose and repair.
Can you service high-efficiency washers?
Yes — our techs are trained on HE washers and follow manufacturer guidelines for repairs and detergents.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit cards, debit, and cash. Payment is collected after repairs are complete unless other arrangements are made.
