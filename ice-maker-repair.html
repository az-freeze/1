Frequently Asked Questions
Why is my ice maker not making ice?
Common causes include water supply issues, faulty inlet valves, clogged filters, or failed ice maker modules. We'll diagnose and repair the correct component.
Why is my ice maker leaking?
Leaks can come from water lines, inlet valves, or improperly seated fill cups. We'll inspect and correct the source of the leak.
Can you replace an ice maker module?
Yes — we can replace ice maker modules and test the full cycle to ensure proper operation.
Do you service built-in and under-counter units?
Yes — we service built-in ice maker units in refrigerators and under-counter ice machines when accessible.
What warranty is included?
We provide parts and labor warranties; terms depend on the repair and parts used.
