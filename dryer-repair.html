Frequently Asked Questions
Why is my dryer not heating?
Common causes include failed heating elements, thermostats, thermal fuses, or blocked vents. We diagnose and replace the faulty component.
Do you repair gas dryers?
Yes — we repair gas dryers and follow strict safety protocols when working on gas components.
How can I improve drying times?
Regularly clean lint filters and vents, avoid overloading the dryer, and ensure the venting system is clear to improve efficiency.
Will you replace drum belts?
Yes. Drum belt replacement is a common repair and we can usually complete it during the visit if the part is on the truck.
What warranty do you offer?
We offer parts and labor warranties on repairs; details depend on the repair and parts used.
