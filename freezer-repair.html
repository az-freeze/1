Frequently Asked Questions
How quickly can you diagnose my freezer?
Most diagnostics are completed within 30–60 minutes on-site. We'll provide repair options and a written estimate.
Is frost buildup a sealed system issue?
Frost can be caused by defrost system failures, door seals, or frequent door openings. Sealed system problems are less common but possible.
Do you replace compressors?
Compressor replacement is sometimes the solution for older units. We evaluate repair cost vs replacement and explain options.
Will you clean condenser coils?
Yes — dirty coils can cause poor cooling. Our service includes checking and cleaning coils when needed.
What warranty do you offer?
We provide a parts and labor warranty; terms depend on the repair and parts used. We'll confirm warranty details before work begins.
