Frequently Asked Questions
Why won't my burner light?
Igniters, gas valves, or clogged ports can prevent ignition. We'll inspect and repair or replace the faulty component.
Is a cracked glass cooktop repairable?
Cracked glass cooktops typically require panel replacement. We'll assess damage and advise on replacement options.
Do you service commercial stoves?
We primarily service residential stoves but can discuss commercial needs on a case-by-case basis.
What if I smell gas?
If you smell gas, leave the area, avoid sparks, and call a qualified technician immediately. We respond quickly to suspected gas leaks.
What warranty do you offer?
We offer parts and labor warranties; terms depend on the repair and parts used.
