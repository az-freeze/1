Frequently Asked Questions
Why won't my oven heat to the set temperature?
Thermostat or temperature sensor failures and heating element issues commonly cause temperature discrepancies. We'll test and replace components as needed.
Can you repair gas ovens?
Yes — our technicians are trained to safely diagnose and repair gas oven components and igniters.
What about self-cleaning oven problems?
Self-clean cycles can overheat certain components. We diagnose any damage and provide repair options.
How long will the repair take?
Most oven repairs are completed within 1–3 hours depending on parts and complexity.
Do you use OEM parts?
We use OEM parts when available or high-quality aftermarket parts when necessary.
